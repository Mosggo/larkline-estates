Selling with Larkline

One agent, from start to sold.

Claire Morgan founded Larkline in 2017. Today, our small team works across Bad Ragaz, with each agent focused on the streets they know best.

We take on fewer clients so the agent who values your home is the same person who handles viewings, answers your questions and sees the sale through.

Before you decide to sell

What should I share when I ask for a valuation?

Your street or neighbourhood and the type of home are enough to start. An agent can ask for more detail when you speak.

Will I work with the same agent throughout?

Yes. The agent who first speaks with you stays with you through viewings, questions and closing.

Does Larkline also handle rentals?
