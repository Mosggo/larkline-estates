Since 2017

How Larkline took shape

The milestones after our founding are illustrative for this prototype.

  1. 2017

    Claire opens Larkline

    Claire Morgan starts an independent agency in Bad Ragaz, with one point of contact for each sale.

  2. 2018

    The first keys change hands

    At an early handover, Claire gets the outgoing owner and the new owners together to go through the home in person.

  3. 2020

    Viewings go online when needed

    The team starts using video walk-throughs for people who can’t travel, so they can ask questions and see the rooms before making the trip.

  4. 2021

    Local coverage grows

    The team assigns agents to different parts of Bad Ragaz, giving clients a contact who can speak about the streets around each property.

  5. 2023

    Neighbourhood walks become part of viewings

    Agents begin setting aside time to walk the nearby streets with buyers who want a feel for day-to-day life in Bad Ragaz.

  6. 2025

    The handover gets its own checklist

    The team gives sellers and buyers a simple list for keys, meter readings and the other details that are easy to miss on moving day.
